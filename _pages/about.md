I am a health economist and postdoctoral research associate in the Division of Health Policy and Insurance Research at the Harvard Pilgrim Health Care Institute. Prior to joining Harvard, I was an Assistant Policy Researcher at RAND, where I also earned my PhD in policy analysis. My research focuses on topics in behavioral health care, medical liability, and population health and has been featured in academic journals, including the _Journal of Public Economics_, _Journal of Health Economics_, and _Health Economics_. 

Please feel free to reach out at yuji_mizushima[at]hphci.harvard.edu or ymizushi[at]rand.org with any questions or collaboration interests.
